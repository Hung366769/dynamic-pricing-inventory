# Data Contract

This project uses `retail_price.csv` as the observed pricing and demand source,
then adds operational tables under `operational_data/` so the notebook can model
a more realistic dynamic pricing and inventory workflow.

## Source Table

### `retail_price.csv`

Observed product-month pricing dataset.

Key fields:

- `product_id`: SKU identifier.
- `product_category_name`: product category.
- `month_year`: monthly period.
- `qty`: observed demand/sales quantity.
- `unit_price`: selling price.
- `lag_price`: previous observed price.
- `comp_1`, `comp_2`, `comp_3`: competitor/reference prices.
- `freight_price`, `customers`, `weekday`, `weekend`, `holiday`, `month`, `year`: operational demand features.

## Generated Operational Tables

Generated by:

```bash
py scripts/generate_operational_datasets.py
```

The generated tables are deterministic and reproducible. They are scenario data
for project simulation, not raw market observations.

### `operational_data/product_costs.csv`

Product-level cost and supplier assumptions.

- `product_id`: SKU identifier.
- `product_category_name`: category.
- `unit_cost`: assumed procurement cost per unit.
- `cost_ratio_to_median_price`: cost divided by the product median selling price.
- `supplier_id`: simulated supplier identifier.
- `min_order_qty`: minimum replenishment quantity.
- `holding_cost_per_unit_month`: monthly inventory holding cost.
- `stockout_penalty_per_unit`: penalty used in inventory/RL simulation.

### `operational_data/inventory_snapshots.csv`

Monthly stock state aligned to `retail_price.csv`.

- `product_id`, `product_category_name`, `month_year`: grain keys.
- `observed_demand`: copied from observed `qty`.
- `opening_stock`: simulated stock available at the start of the month.
- `fulfilled_qty`: demand fulfilled by available stock.
- `lost_sales`: demand not fulfilled because of stockout.
- `ending_stock`: stock after demand and any replenishment action.
- `inventory_position`: stock position used by reorder policy.
- `reorder_point`: reorder threshold.
- `safety_stock`: buffer stock.
- `lead_time_weeks`: supplier lead time.
- `stockout_flag`: `1` when observed demand exceeds opening stock.
- `sell_through_rate`: fulfilled units divided by opening stock.

### `operational_data/purchase_orders.csv`

Simulated replenishment events triggered when inventory position falls below the
reorder point.

- `purchase_order_id`: order identifier.
- `product_id`, `product_category_name`: item being replenished.
- `order_date`: simulated purchase order creation date.
- `arrival_date`: simulated arrival date.
- `quantity_ordered`: replenishment quantity.
- `supplier_id`: supplier from `product_costs.csv`.
- `lead_time_weeks`: supplier lead time.
- `unit_cost`: procurement cost per unit.

## Modeling Use

- Real observed data: price, demand, competitor price, product features.
- Scenario operational data: costs, inventory, lead time, safety stock, purchase orders.
- Main model: supervised ML demand forecasting plus hybrid price-grid optimization.
- RL model: simulation extension for sequential pricing policy comparison.

Important wording for reports: inventory and replenishment tables are generated
operational scenario data. They make the IT system workflow realistic, but they
should not be described as raw observed inventory records.
